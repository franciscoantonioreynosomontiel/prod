Our Philosophy

Dedicated to the meticulous tactile feedback of vintage metalwork.

Modern plastic components are meant to be thrown away. Metal parts, forged through labor and flame, are designed to outlive generations. At Valve and Manifold, we specialize in breathing new life into blocks that have rested in rust for decades.

"The exact click of a lifter matching a camshaft lobe is not a calculation; it is a musical symphony that only a human ear, honed over decades of mechanical dedication, can master."

— Master Carver of Cylinders

By limiting our restoration projects to just a handful of masterpieces annually, we secure enough focus to blueprint every minute mechanical connection. We inspect valves using blue-dying paste, custom hand-lap seats, and align combustion chambers with micrometer-level tactile feedback.
